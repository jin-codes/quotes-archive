# Quotes Archive

A website for collecting quotes that stay with you, and for stumbling on new ones at random.

🔗 **https://quotes-archive.vercel.app**

## Features

- **Surprise me** — shuffles the collection and shows one quote at a time.
- **Favorite** — save the quotes you love and shuffle through just those.
- **Language** — use the **KOR** / **ENG** buttons to show only Korean or English quotes. Shuffling stays within the selected language.
- **Tags** — browse by topic tag, and shuffle only within the tags you pick.
- **Download** — export the quote list as an xlsx file.

> ⚠️ When not logged in, refreshing the page resets all settings (language, tags, favorites, etc.).

## When logged in

- Your favorites are saved to your account.
- Regular accounts can't add, edit, or delete quotes directly. Instead, you can send the administrator an add/edit/delete **request**.
